01 THE IDEA

Not everything needs an answer. Sometimes, it just needs to be understood.

We write for different reasons. To remember something, to process an experience, or simply to get a thought out of our heads. I've been curious about what happens when we combine that simple human habit with artificial intelligence. Could technology help us recognise patterns in our thoughts and understand our experiences differently? SoulNote is my exploration of that possibility.

03 THE AI VISION

What if your diary could remember the bigger picture?

Imagine writing something today and having an AI that can connect it to a thought you recorded weeks ago. Not because it knows everything about you, but because it has access to relevant context that helps it understand the conversation better.

I'm exploring Large Language Models, contextual memory and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to see how this might work in practice.
